## Ki67 and local recurrence

Ki67 was scored on archival tumour blocks for 640 patients. High Ki67 was
associated with local recurrence (hazard ratio 2.1, 95% CI 1.4 to 3.2,
p = 0.0004). The cut-point of 14% gave the strongest separation between the
high and low groups. Ki67 remained significant after adjusting for grade.
