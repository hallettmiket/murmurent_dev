\section{Discussion}

Stromal MMP11 was among the strongest predictors of progression in our
cohort. This is consistent with what is known about the protein. MMP11
(stromelysin-3) was first identified as a gene expressed specifically in
stromal cells surrounding invasive breast carcinomas~\cite{basset1990}, and
its overexpression in the stroma of ductal carcinoma \emph{in situ} has since
been reported~\cite{moreau2012}. Stromal MMP11 is raised in 68\% of DCIS
lesions~\cite{stats2026}, so the signal we observe is likely to generalise
beyond our cohort.

Several mechanisms could link stromal MMP11 to progression. Tumour-derived
exosomes induce cancer-associated fibroblast properties in mammary
fibroblasts~\cite{zhu2022}, and MMP11 rises sharply at the transition from
DCIS to invasive disease~\cite{laurent2017}. Together these suggest that
stromal activation precedes invasion and that MMP11 marks it.
