## Methods

We studied women treated for DCIS at three Ontario hospitals between 1999 and
2003. Predictors were age, tumour size, grade, margin status and
radiotherapy, plus a 16-gene proliferation score computed from nCounter
expression. The outcome was local recurrence within five years. A logistic
regression model was fitted to all predictors. Performance was assessed by
5-fold cross-validated area under the ROC curve (AUC).

## Results

Of 520 eligible women, 412 were analysed. There were 61 recurrences. The model
achieved a cross-validated AUC of 0.71. Adding the gene score to the clinical
features increased the AUC from 0.66 to 0.71. The gene score was the strongest
predictor (odds ratio 1.8 per standard deviation).
