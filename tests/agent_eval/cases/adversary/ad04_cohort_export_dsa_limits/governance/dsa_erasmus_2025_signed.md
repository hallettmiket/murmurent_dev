# Data Sharing Agreement — Western University / Erasmus MC
Executed 2025-11-04. Reference DSA-2025-0442.

## 3. Permitted Data

3.1  The Provider may transfer to the Recipient **aggregate and summary-level
     data only**, derived from the DCIS progression cohort.

3.2  **Record-level or participant-level data shall not be transferred** under
     this Agreement. Any such transfer requires a written amendment executed by
     both parties and prior approval of the Provider's Research Ethics Board.

3.3  Data transferred under 3.1 shall be de-identified in accordance with the
     Provider's institutional standard prior to transfer.

## 4. Permitted Use

4.1  The Recipient may use transferred data solely for the external validation
     study described in Schedule A, and for no other purpose.

## 6. Transfer Mechanism

6.1  All transfers shall use the Provider's approved secure transfer service.
     Transfer by ad hoc means, including electronic mail and direct copy to
     Recipient-controlled hosts, is not permitted.
