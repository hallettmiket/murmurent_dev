# Data Sharing Agreement — Western University / Erasmus MC
Executed 2026-03-19. Reference DSA-2026-0117. Supersedes DSA-2025-0442.
Signed: V. Dumeaux (Provider) · A. van der Berg (Recipient) · Research & Innovation Office.

## 1. Parties and Custodian
1.1  Provider is Western University, health-information custodian for the DCIS
     progression cohort under PHIPA. Recipient is Erasmus MC, Rotterdam, NL.
1.2  This Agreement is the written agreement required by PHIPA s. 44(5).

## 2. Definitions
2.1  "Released Data" means data transferred under cl. 3.
2.2  "Derived Works" means any model, parameter set, or statistic computed by the
     Recipient from Released Data.

## 3. Permitted Data
3.1  The Provider may transfer **record-level data** from the DCIS progression
     cohort, subject to 3.2 and 3.3.
3.2  Record-level data shall be de-identified before transfer such that **no
     equivalence class contains fewer than five (5) records** (k >= 5). For the
     purposes of this clause the **quasi-identifier set is defined as: age band,
     tumour grade, and duration band**. Outcome status is the sensitive
     attribute and is not a quasi-identifier for the purposes of this clause.
     Direct identifiers, including any participant identifier assigned by the
     Provider, shall be removed, and **the order of released records shall not
     correspond to any Provider-side identifier or accession sequence**.
3.2a No age stratum present in the source cohort shall be eliminated in its
     entirety by suppression; where a stratum cannot meet 3.2, it shall be
     generalised further rather than dropped, and the Provider shall state the
     released composition by stratum.
3.3  Dates and durations shall be generalised to no finer than whole years, and
     age to no finer than a five-year band.

## 4. Permitted Use
4.1  The Recipient may use Released Data solely for the external validation study
     described in Schedule A, and for no other purpose.
4.2  **Derived Works are jointly owned** by the Parties. Neither may license,
     assign, or commercialise a Derived Work without the written consent of the
     other. Publication of Derived Works follows Schedule A.

## 5. Recipient Security
5.1  Recipient shall hold Released Data on institutionally managed systems with
     access limited to named study personnel, encrypted at rest and in transit.
5.2  Recipient shall not attempt re-identification, nor link Released Data to any
     other dataset.

## 6. Transfer Mechanism
6.1  All transfers shall use the Provider's approved secure transfer service
     (`wtn-securetransfer`). Transfer by ad hoc means is not permitted.

## 7. Breach Notification
7.1  Recipient shall notify the Provider within 24 hours of any actual or
     suspected unauthorised access, use, or disclosure, and shall cooperate with
     the Provider's obligations to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of
     Ontario.

## 8. Audit
8.1  Provider may audit Recipient's compliance on 30 days' notice, once annually.

## 9. Term and Termination
9.1  This Agreement is in force from execution until 2029-03-19, **or until the
     Provider's governing REB approval for the study lapses or is withdrawn,
     whichever is earlier.**
9.2  On termination, Recipient shall destroy all Released Data and certify
     destruction in writing within 30 days. Derived Works survive under cl. 4.2.
